## Audit rules

1. The evidence boundary is **November 1, 2025 at 12:00 a.m. ET**.
2. Acknowledgment, referral/introduction, recruiter reply, interview invitation, and interview completed are separate stages.
3. An application reaches interview stage only when a recruiter proposed or confirmed a **specific call or interview time**.
4. Automated acknowledgments do not count as interviews.
5. An application whose rejection explicitly cites a missing required qualification is excluded from interview-stage totals in full.
6. Strategy 1 gets credit only when a qualifying interview-stage thread explicitly references the PhD research.
7. Strategy 2 gets credit only when a qualifying interview-stage thread explicitly references an AWS capstone project or GitHub link.
8. Employer-fit means Citi, or another company whose primary business is banking, capital markets, insurance underwriting, or cybersecurity/technology-risk consulting.
9. Healthcare, industrials, retail, consumer goods, and general enterprise technology companies are not employer-fit merely because a role title contains security, risk, or audit.
10. Controlled subsidiaries are counted when the Fortune 500 parent relationship is clear.
