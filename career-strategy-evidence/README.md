# Career Strategy Evidence

**Audit date:** September 22, 2026  
**Evidence window:** November 1, 2025 at 12:00 a.m. ET through September 22, 2026  
**Purpose:** Measure whether the actual Fortune 500 application record shows an interview-conversion advantage for (1) continued PhD research or (2) continued AWS/cloud development showcased through QCC capstone work.

## Verdict

Based on 25 Fortune 500 application acknowledgments from November 1, 2025 through September 22, 2026, both strategies remain necessary: each has a qualifying interview-stage count of zero, and the QCC AWS capstone is not currently public in the verified GitHub repository, so neither strategy has yet demonstrated a measurable interview-conversion advantage.

## Audit rules

1. The focused record begins at 12:00 a.m. ET on November 1, 2025 and ends on September 22, 2026.
2. Acknowledgment, referral/introduction, recruiter reply, interview invitation, and interview completed are recorded as separate stages.
3. An application counts as interview-stage only when a recruiter proposed or confirmed a specific date/time for a call or interview.
4. An automated application acknowledgment is not interview-stage.
5. If a rejection explicitly cites a missing required qualification, that application is excluded from interview-stage totals in full.
6. Strategy 1 receives credit only if a qualifying interview-stage thread explicitly references the PhD research.
7. Strategy 2 receives credit only if a qualifying interview-stage thread explicitly references an AWS capstone project or GitHub link.
8. A single application may receive credit for both strategies, one strategy, or neither.
9. Employer-fit means Citigroup, or another company whose primary business is banking, capital markets, insurance underwriting, or cybersecurity/technology-risk consulting. Healthcare, industrials, retail, consumer goods, and general enterprise technology companies do not qualify.
10. Controlled subsidiaries of a Fortune 500 parent are included in the application count when the parent relationship is clear.

**Next test:** Publish the QCC/GridVision work with a clear README, architecture, personal-contribution statement, security controls, deployment evidence, and a live demo link; then track recruiter mentions of that link.
